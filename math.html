What is Modulus (|x|) and Why Does it Exist?

1. The Mechanical Job: The "Minus-Eraser Machine"

Whatever you feed into | … | comes out non-negative. It leaves positive numbers and zero alone, and simply strips away the minus sign from negative ones:

|5| = 5 • |0| = 0 • |-5| = 5

Why do we need this? (Real-World Use Cases)

The Smartwatch / Step Counter

Take 4 steps forward: +4.
Take 4 steps backward: -4.

Your smartwatch doesn't say "you walked 0 steps." It only cares about how much movement happened, not the direction: |-4| = 4 steps.

Distance Between Two Locations

House A is at km 2. House B is at km 10.
2 - 10 = -8.

You can't say "they are minus 8 km apart." Wrapping it in modulus |2 - 10| = 8 means you never have to worry about which number is bigger.

Engineering "Margin of Error"

A factory part must be 10 mm, with at most 0.2 mm error:

Instead of two separate equations, engineers write one clean statement: |x - 10| ≤ 0.2 (the difference from 10, positive or negative, must not exceed 0.2).

Why does |x| = 5 have two answers?

When an exam asks to solve |x| = 5, treat it as a riddle:

"I had a secret number x. I passed it through the minus-eraser machine, and 5 came out. What could my secret number have been?"
• Maybe it was +5 (because |5| = 5).
• Maybe it was -5 (because |-5| = 5).
Therefore: x = 5 or x = -5.

The Basket Concept: What is a Set? (Kas yra aibė?)

Think of a set (Aibė) as a labeled basket holding distinct numbers or objects. If a number is inside, we write x ∈ A (belongs to). If not, x ∉ A.

The Three Fundamental Set Operations

1. Sąjunga (∪) — Union

"Combine everything." Combine basket A and basket B together. No duplicates.

A ∪ B = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
2. Sankirta (∩) — Intersection

"Shared items only." Numbers that are in BOTH basket A and basket B at the same time.

A ∩ B = {3, 4}
3. Skirtumas (\) — Difference

"Take away set B." Start with set A and remove any item that is also in set B.

A \ B = {1, 2}

Užduotis 24: The Hopscotch Number Lists

Goal: Find which numbers appear in both baskets without confusing equations.

Just write down the numbers and spot the matches:

Basket A (jumps by +3): 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29, 32, 35, 38, 41, 44, 47...
Basket B (jumps by +5): 7, 12, 17, 22, 27, 32, 37, 42, 47, 52, 57...
Basket C (just odd numbers): 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19...
  1. a) A ∩ B (Numbers in BOTH A and B):
    Look at the highlighted matches: 17, 32, 47, 62, 77... (They jump by +15 each step!)
  2. b) A ∩ C (Numbers in Basket A that are ODD):
    Look at List A and circle the odd ones: 5, 11, 17, 23, 29... (They jump by +6 each step!)
  3. c) A ∩ B ∩ C (Numbers in all three):
    Look at your matches from part (a) and keep only the ODD ones:
    17 is odd (✓), 32 is even (✗), 47 is odd (✓), 62 is even (✗)...
    → 17, 47, 77, 107... (They jump by +30 each step!)
  4. d) (A ∩ B) ∪ C (Combine them into one big basket):
    Dump Basket C (all odd numbers) and the matches from A ∩ B into one pile.
    The odd ones (17, 47, 77...) were already in C! So you get all odd numbers ≥ 3, plus the even numbers {32, 62, 92, ...}.

Užduotis 20: The Two Mini-Riddles

Text: "Aibė A yra sprendiniai lygties 19 - 3x = -8, o aibė B yra sprendiniai lygties x² - 81 = 0."

Basket A: What is x in 19 - 3x = -8?

Think backwards: 19 minus what gives -8?
• 19 - 27 = -8. So 3x = 27.
• What number times 3 is 27? x = 9.
So Basket A has just one number: A = {9}.

Basket B: What is x in x² - 81 = 0?

This asks: "What number times itself equals 81?"
• 9 × 9 = 81.
• And remember: (-9) × (-9) = 81 too!
So Basket B has two numbers: B = {-9, 9}.

  1. A ∪ B: Combine both → {-9, 9}
  2. A ∩ B: Shared number → {9}
  3. A \ B: Remove 9 from A → ∅ (Empty set!)
  4. B \ A: Remove 9 from B → {-9}

Exponents & Roots (Laipsniai ir Šaknys)

The Golden Rule of Fractional Powers: "The Root Tree"

Whenever you see a fraction in the exponent, like xm/n:

• The bottom number (n) is the ROOT (underground → √[n]).
• The top number (m) is the POWER (up in the branches).
Example: 1252/3 = (√[3]{125})² = 5² = 25!

1. The Negative Exponent Flip

A minus in the exponent just flips it downstairs into a fraction:

x-2 = 1 / x²
(1/25)-0.5 = 250.5 = √25 = 5
2. Multiplying & Dividing

Same base? Just add or subtract exponents:

ap • aq = ap + q
xp : xq = xp - q
3. Conjugates: (A - B)(A + B)

Whenever you see twin square roots with opposite signs, use difference of squares:

(√15 - √6)(√15 + √6) = 15 - 6 = 9

Solved Homework (New!) — The Intuitive Guide

Every problem assigned for homework (2a, 2b • 5a, 5c • 6a, 6c, 6e, 6g), explained using the 3 Golden Rules so you actually understand why it works.

The 3 Secret Rules to Solve All Homework Problems:

Rule 1: The Root Tree (Fractions in Power)

• Top number = power in branches.
• Bottom number = root underground (√).
91/2 = √9 = 3 • 1251/3 = √[3]{125} = 5.

Rule 2: The Elevator (Minus in Power)

A minus in the power never makes a number negative! It just flips it downstairs into the basement:
x-1 = 1/x • (1/5)-1 = 5.

Rule 3: Power Arithmetic

• When multiplying (•) → ADD powers.
• When dividing (:) → SUBTRACT powers.
• When stacked (a²)³ → MULTIPLY powers (a6).

Užduotis 2a: Calculating Shared Roots

Expression: 61/2 • 181/2 • 21/2

The Simple Intuition:

Every single number has the exact same power 1/2! That means all three live under square root houses (√). We can combine them all under one master roof!

  1. Step 1 (Combine under one root):
    Since they all share the power 1/2:
    61/2 • 181/2 • 21/2 = √(6 × 18 × 2).
  2. Step 2 (Spot the easy multiplication):
    18 × 2 = 36.
    So inside the root we have: √(6 × 36).
  3. Step 3 (Pull out the clean square):
    What is √36? Exactly 6!
    The other 6 stays inside the root: 6√6.
Answer: 6√6
Užduotis 2b: The Twin House (Conjugates)

Expression: (√15 - √6)1/2 • (√15 + √6)1/2

The Simple Intuition:

Both big brackets have the exact same power of 1/2! That means we can multiply the insides together first, under one master roof.

  1. Step 1 (Combine into one master bracket):
    [(√15 - √6)(√15 + √6)]1/2
  2. Step 2 (The Difference of Squares magic trick):
    Whenever you have (A - B)(A + B), the middle terms cancel out completely, leaving just A² - B²!
    (√15)² - (√6)² = 15 - 6 = 9!
  3. Step 3 (Apply the master power of 1/2):
    91/2 = √9 = 3!
    (Look how clean this turns out: a scary mess of roots turns into the single number 3!)
Answer: 3
Užduotis 5a: Combine Into a Single Power

Expression: a1/6 • a5

The Simple Intuition (Rule 3):

Both parts have the exact same base letter a and are being multiplied. So you simply ADD the powers: 1/6 + 5.

  1. Think of pizzas: If a pizza has 6 slices, 5 whole pizzas equals 5 × 6 = 30 slices:
    5 = 30/6.
  2. Add the fractions:
    1/6 + 30/6 = 31/6.
Answer: a31/6 (or a5 1/6)
Užduotis 5c: Multiply and Divide Exponents

Expression: b1/6 • b5/9 : b-3/4

The Simple Intuition (Rule 3):

Multiply (•) means ADD (+).
Divide (:) means SUBTRACT (-).

  1. Write out the power math:
    Total Power = 1/6 + 5/9 - (-3/4)
  2. Minus a minus becomes a plus:
    Total Power = 1/6 + 5/9 + 3/4
  3. Find the common denominator:
    What number can 6, 9, and 4 all divide into evenly? 36!
    • 1/6 = 6/36
    • 5/9 = 20/36
    • 3/4 = 27/36
  4. Add all the numerators:
    (6 + 20 + 27) / 36 = 53/36.
Answer: b53/36 (or b1 17/36)
Užduotis 6a: The Negative Exponent Drop

Expression: 5 • 125-1/3

  1. Use Rule 2 (The Elevator): The minus in 125-1/3 flips it downstairs into the denominator:
    125-1/3 = 1 / 1251/3.
  2. Use Rule 1 (The Root Tree): A power of 1/3 means cube root (√[3]{}):
    What number × itself × itself = 125? 5! (since 5 × 5 × 5 = 125).
    So 1251/3 = 5, which makes 125-1/3 = 1/5.
  3. Finish the multiplication:
    5 × (1/5) = 1!
Answer: 1
Užduotis 6c: Stacked Powers & Decimal Fractions

Expression: (4-1/5)-30 : 0.26

  1. Step 1 (Stacked powers multiply!):
    (-1/5) × (-30) = +30/5 = 6.
    So the entire first piece collapses into simply: 46!
  2. Step 2 (Turn the decimal 0.2 into a fraction):
    0.2 = 2/10 = 1/5.
    So 0.26 = (1/5)6 = 1 / 56.
  3. Step 3 (Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal):
    46 : (1 / 56) = 46 • 56!
  4. Step 4 (Combine under the same power 6):
    (4 × 5)6 = 206 = 64,000,000!
Answer: 64,000,000
Užduotis 6e: Tiny Decimal Division

Expression: 813/4 : 0.000014/5

  1. Solve 813/4:
    • Bottom 4 means 4th root: what times itself 4 times is 81? That's 3 (since 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81).
    • Top 3 means power: 3³ = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27.
  2. Solve 0.000014/5:
    • Count the decimal places: 0.00001 has 5 places, so it is 10-5 (or 1/100,000).
    • Raise it to 4/5: (10-5)4/5 = 10(-5 × 4/5) = 10-4 = 1 / 10,000.
  3. Divide them:
    27 : (1 / 10,000) = 27 × 10,000 = 270,000!
Answer: 270,000
Užduotis 6g: The Triple Cube Root Workout

Expression: 0.008-1/3 - 0.064-2/3 + 0.125-4/3

The Secret Code:

Look closely at the three decimals. Every single one is a perfect cube!
• 0.008 = 0.2³ = (1/5)³
• 0.064 = 0.4³ = (2/5)³
• 0.125 = 0.5³ = (1/2)³

  1. Piece 1:
    [(1/5)³]-1/3 = (1/5)-1. Flip the fraction (Rule 2) → 5.
  2. Piece 2:
    [(2/5)³]-2/3 = (2/5)-2. Flip and square: (5/2)² = 25/4 = 6.25.
  3. Piece 3:
    [(1/2)³]-4/3 = (1/2)-4. Flip and power to 4: 24 = 16.
  4. Combine all three pieces:
    5 - 6.25 + 16 = 21 - 6.25 = 14.75 (or as a fraction: 59/4)!
Answer: 14.75 (or 59/4)

Cube Formulas & Simplifying Rational Fractions

From textbook page 32: factoring polynomials and canceling twin terms.

The Two Cube Factoring Formulas (Kūbų formulės)

• Sum of cubes: a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² - ab + b²)
• Difference of cubes: a³ - b³ = (a - b)(a² + ab + b²)

Example 3a: Canceling Common Factors in Fractions

Simplify: (b + 2) / (b³ + 8)

  1. Notice that 8 = 2³, so the bottom is a sum of cubes: b³ + 2³.
  2. Expand the bottom using the formula: (b + 2)(b² - 2b + 4).
  3. Now rewrite the fraction:
    (b + 2) / [(b + 2)(b² - 2b + 4)]
  4. Cancel the matching twin (b + 2) on top and bottom!
  5. Final Answer: 1 / (b² - 2b + 4).
Example 3d: The Multi-Step Fraction Workout

Simplify: [7(x - 2) / (x³ - 8) - (x + 2) / (x² + 2x + 4)] • (2x² + 4x + 8) / (x - 3)

  1. Factor x³ - 8 = (x - 2)(x² + 2x + 4).
  2. The first term cancels (x - 2): it becomes 7 / (x² + 2x + 4)!
  3. Now both terms inside the brackets share the exact same denominator!
    [7 - (x + 2)] / (x² + 2x + 4) = (5 - x) / (x² + 2x + 4).
  4. Factor a 2 out of the multiplier: 2(x² + 2x + 4) / (x - 3).
  5. Cancel the big quadratic twin (x² + 2x + 4) on top and bottom!
  6. Final Answer: 2(5 - x) / (x - 3) = (10 - 2x) / (x - 3).
